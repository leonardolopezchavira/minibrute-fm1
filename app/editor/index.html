MB-1 patch editor

Every value of an MB-1 patch on one sheet, drawn like the paper patch sheets MiniBrute players fill in and share. Drag a control up or down (Shift for fine), scroll over it, or use the arrow keys; double-click puts it back to INIT. Play with the keys below or your computer's keyboard (A–K, Z/X octave). Connect the FM-1 over USB to read its patches, hear your edits on it as you make them, and store them in its slots (Chrome or Edge).
