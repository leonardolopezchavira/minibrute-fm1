MB-1 on the SMK-37

For the M-Vave SMK-37 Pro and the Donner Starrykey 37 Play (the same keyboard), with builds for the SMK-37 Elite and the MKE-P37. Version {{VERSION}}.

Preview

This is MB-1 brought up on the keyboard: the FM-1's MB-1, with the SMK-37's keys, wheels and pedal. Its own panel (the faders as the oscillator mixer, eight knobs for the Brute controls, the pads) comes next. It has not been tried on a keyboard yet: the SMK-37 Elite and MKE-P37 builds may never be.

Install

Connect the keyboard by USB (switched on, as it is), open the installer for your model in Chrome or Edge and press Install.

SMK-37 Pro / Starrykey 37 Play SMK-37 Elite MKE-P37

MB-1 keeps its patches and settings in the keyboard's own store (16 of its sectors); it never writes the keyboard's calibration, its Bluetooth data or its stock presets. Back to M-Vave's firmware: install it with M-Vave's updater, as for any update.

A full backup of the keyboard's flash first is optional; it is the same keyboard as SQ-80's, so its steps (Windows, about ten minutes, once) are on SQ-80's SMK-37 page.

The controls

K1Volume
K2 · K3 · K4As the FM-1's SELECT (every page in turn) · ALGO (the mod wheel) · PRESET (the patch slot)
K5–K8The page's four parameters: the screen names them K5–K8
KeysAll 37 play (C3–C6, OCTAVE − / + move them), with their velocity
Wheels · pedalPitch bend (the patch's BEND range) · the mod wheel · sustain
PadsFor now, the white keys of the FM-1's two octaves
ButtonsAs on the FM-1: PATCH = HOME (three times: RANDOM), SCALE = SEL, CHORD = ENV, NOTE REPEAT = LFO, PARA = EDIT, FX, GLOBE = GLO, BT = SAVE, ARP, SEQ, SEQ PLAY = PLAY, SEQ REC = REC

The rest works as the manual says for the FM-1; the patches, the .syx files and the patch editor are the same.

The SMK-37 support builds on genkigenki's fm1-to-smk37 kit and SMK-37 layer (GPL-3.0), by way of SQ-80's copy of fm1-base.
