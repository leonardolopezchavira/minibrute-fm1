MB-1 on the SMK-37

An aggressive monophonic virtual analog synthesizer for the M-Vave SMK-37 Pro and the Donner Starrykey 37 Play (the same keyboard), with builds for the SMK-37 Elite and the MKE-P37.

What you get

The same MB-1 as on the FM-1 (the same sound, the 128 patches, the factory bank, the arpeggiator, the sequencer and the effects), on a keyboard with more to hold on to: 37 keys with velocity, a panel of eight knobs and four faders laid out after the MiniBrute's, 16 pads that show and pick the sequencer's steps, pitch and mod wheels and a sustain pedal jack. The patches, the .syx files and the patch editor are the FM-1's.

Tried on an SMK-37 Pro. The Donner Starrykey 37 Play is the same keyboard; the SMK-37 Elite and MKE-P37 builds have not been tried on a keyboard.

Install

Connect the keyboard by USB (switched on, as it is), open the installer for your model in Chrome or Edge and press Install. Updates: the same installer, the same way; your patches, patterns and settings stay.

The panel

KnobsAt power-on (KNOB BANK lit): K1 Ultrasaw · K2 Metalizer · K3 Brute Factor · K4 Cutoff · K5 Resonance · K6 Env amount · K7 Decay/Release (the amp's and the filter's decay and release together, the patch's shape kept) · K8 LFO rate. The value shows at the top of the screen as you turn.
KNOB BANKSwitches the knobs to the pages, as on the FM-1 (K1 volume, K2 SELECT, K3 ALGO: the mod wheel, K4 PRESET, K5–K8 the page's four, which the screen names K5–K8), and back
FadersFADER BANK steps A–D (lit past A): A the oscillator mixer (saw, square, triangle, sub) · B the filter envelope · C the amp envelope · D noise, distortion, delay and reverb mix. A fader takes over a value once it reaches it, so nothing jumps on a patch change: until then the screen says UP TO or DOWN TO.
< >The patch before, the next (on the piano roll: its steps)
VolumeK1 on the pages; the foot of the screen shows it (VOL), the top as it changes. 50 % at power-on.
KeysAll 37 play (C3–C6) with their velocity; OCTAVE − / + move them three octaves either way (C0–C9: an 88-key piano's range and more)
Wheels · pedalPitch bend (the patch's BEND range) · the mod wheel · sustain
PadsThe white keys of the FM-1's two octaves; the sequencer's steps while it plays and on the piano roll
Key testGLOBE (GLO) pressed again: the keybed measured as you play. Each key's two contacts live (dim: the first, orange: both), the presses with the time from the first contact to the second (what velocity is taken from), grazes (the first contact only: no note) and half lifts (a key lifted past the second contact only and pressed again: no new note), the fastest repeat of one key and between two, and the last events. FX starts it again.
ButtonsAs on the FM-1: PATCH = HOME (three times: RANDOM), SCALE = SEL, CHORD = ENV, NOTE REPEAT = LFO, PARA = EDIT, FX, GLOBE = GLO, BT = SAVE, ARP, SEQ, SEQ PLAY = PLAY, SEQ REC = REC

MB-1 starts on A01 at power-on, as stored.

The sequencer

The FM-1's, after the KeyStep's: SEQ its page, SEQ PLAY starts and stops it, SEQ REC arms recording (stopped: a key a step, FX a rest, FX with a key held a tie; playing: in time). SEQ REC held a second clears the pattern. While it plays, the keys transpose the pattern (MicroBrute style): a key shifts it by its distance from the pattern's first note, until the next key. While it plays the pads show its steps, 16 at a time: the step playing orange, notes white, ties dim orange.

Piano rollSEQ pressed again. The pattern by pitch, 16 steps at a time, its rows a keyboard's (white keys' rows grey, black keys' dark, each C named); the step playing marked above, the step picked framed and named at the top
PadsPick a step (with REC armed and stopped, the next key, FX or FX with a key goes into it)
< >The 16 steps before or after: 1–16, 17–32, 33–48, 49–64 (past the pattern's end too: a step picked there makes it longer)
K5 · K6The step's note (a rest becomes a note; heard as it changes) · its velocity
