MB-1 on the SMK-37

For the M-Vave SMK-37 Pro and the Donner Starrykey 37 Play (the same keyboard), with builds for the SMK-37 Elite and the MKE-P37. Version {{VERSION}}.

Preview

MB-1 on the keyboard, with a panel of its own: the faders as the oscillator mixer and eight knobs for the Brute controls. The pads come next. Tried on an SMK-37 Pro; the SMK-37 Elite and MKE-P37 builds have not been.

Install

Connect the keyboard by USB (switched on, as it is), open the installer for your model in Chrome or Edge and press Install.

SMK-37 Pro / Starrykey 37 Play SMK-37 Elite MKE-P37

MB-1 keeps its patches and settings in the keyboard's own store (16 of its sectors); it never writes the keyboard's calibration, its Bluetooth data or its stock presets. Back to M-Vave's firmware: install it with M-Vave's updater, as for any update.

A full backup of the keyboard's flash first is optional; it is the same keyboard as SQ-80's, so its steps (Windows, about ten minutes, once) are on SQ-80's SMK-37 page.

The controls

Knobs (the panel)At power-on: K1 Ultrasaw · K2 Metalizer · K3 Brute Factor · K4 Cutoff · K5 Resonance · K6 Env amount · K7 Decay/Release (the amp's and the filter's decay and release together) · K8 LFO rate. The value shows at the top of the screen as you turn.
KNOB BANKLit: the panel. Pressed: the knobs as on the FM-1 (K1 volume, K2 SELECT, K3 ALGO (the mod wheel), K4 PRESET, K5–K8 the page's four, which the screen names K5–K8); again, the panel
FadersFADER BANK steps A–D (lit past A): A the oscillator mixer (saw, square, triangle, sub) · B the filter envelope · C the amp envelope · D noise, distortion, delay and reverb mix. A fader takes over a value once it reaches it (no jumps on a patch change): until then the screen says UP TO or DOWN TO.
< >The patch before, the next
VolumeK1 on the pages (KNOB BANK); the foot of the screen shows it (VOL), and the top as it changes. It starts at 50 % at power-on.
SequencerAs on the FM-1 (SEQ, PLAY, REC; SEQ REC held a second clears the pattern). While it plays, the pads show its steps, 16 at a time: the step playing orange, notes white, ties dim orange. SEQ pressed again: the piano roll, the pattern drawn by pitch; there the pads pick a step (with REC armed and stopped, the next key, FX for a rest, or FX with a key held for a tie, goes into it), and SEQ held with < or > shows the 16 steps before or after. Each step's note is named under it. The knobs edit the step picked: K5 its note (a rest becomes a note), K6 its velocity, K7 which step (one past the end adds a step), K8 NOTE, TIE or REST; FX deletes it (a rest). HOME (or any page button) goes back.
KeysAll 37 play (C3–C6; OCTAVE − / + move them three octaves either way: C0–C9, an 88-key piano's range and more), with their velocity
Wheels · pedalPitch bend (the patch's BEND range) · the mod wheel · sustain
PadsThe white keys of the FM-1's two octaves; the sequencer's steps while it plays and on the piano roll (below)
ButtonsAs on the FM-1: PATCH = HOME (three times: RANDOM), SCALE = SEL, CHORD = ENV, NOTE REPEAT = LFO, PARA = EDIT, FX, GLOBE = GLO, BT = SAVE, ARP, SEQ, SEQ PLAY = PLAY, SEQ REC = REC

The rest works as the manual says for the FM-1; the patches, the .syx files and the patch editor are the same.

The SMK-37 support builds on genkigenki's fm1-to-smk37 kit and SMK-37 layer (GPL-3.0), by way of SQ-80's copy of fm1-base.
